\subsection*{(a) Real-world and machine-learning problem}
\begin{quote}
How can student background information and first-year academic results help
distinguish recorded dropout from graduation and inform academic advisors'
prioritization of student support?
\end{quote}

The dataset is described by Realinho et al.~\cite{realinho2022}.
\emph{Writing prompt: connect binary classification and dropout ranking to an
advisor's limited appointment capacity, using 20\% as an illustrative budget.
Define dropout as the positive class. State the original 4,424 records and the
3,630 resolved outcomes used after excluding 794 \texttt{Enrolled} records.}

\emph{Writing prompt: explain that both semesters imply an assumed end-of-first-year
decision point. Missing dropout dates and uncertain measurement times limit this
to retrospective feasibility; prediction metrics cannot show prevented dropouts.}
